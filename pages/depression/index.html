depression

oct 26, 2026
what do i know?
  1. i am experiencing a period of acute depression.
    it is likely triggered by the breakup with a, but caused by more than that.
  2. it’s exhausting, oppressive, and painful.
  3. it feels like it’s been going on for a very long time.
  4. sometimes there are periods (~4-24+ hours) where it feels untolerable.
    it is unclear to me what triggers these specific periods.
  5. i’ve struggled with chronic depression in the past, and will continue to do so.
  6. it feels like this reaction is outsized to the breakup.
  7. i am in therapy, and i meet with a psych NP.
  8. the episodes are unpredictable, and highly disruptive to my life.
    this is frustrating.
  9. i’ve experienced feelings like this before, to this severity, and i have gotten better.

why is this happening, and why now?
  1. unclear, honestly
  2. other contributing factors:
  • i don’t have a good support system, or a community to rely on
  • i feel very alone, and very lonely
  • i live alone
  • things are work were tumultuous; i almost rage quit a few times.
  • i felt very purposeless and unanchored
  • a had provided: an anchor, a deep sense of companionship and comfort of being myself, affection, laughter, stimulation, and the inroads towards a friends and a community that i liked

how are things now?
  1. i still do not many friends whom i love being around, and who bring me joy and laughter and a sense of fun and companionship, in new york. i am still very alone.
  2. things are shifting at work.
  3. prior / in-midst of the disruption i was thinking about:
  • getting tango shoes, and trying tango more deliberately
  • possibly getting more involved with the queer tango crew
  • a life direction; a career change – things have moved a bit more here
  • working through my apartment (shelving, cleaning, rearranging, reducing down– )

many of these things are still relevant. it feels – a little more clear.

re-orienting my life around reading and writing, and illustrating – that feels like the biggest thing.

then, work – maintaining my standing, keeping my momentum, riding the wave of good-enough and interest so i don’t burn out.
then, to slowly piece out the financials, the practical bits, the plan..

then, the apartment, the tango, the various random social events, the trying out new things – not to say these are less important or less present, but i would not revolve my life around these things.

and then, somewhere around there – orthogonal, because as necessary as air – the effort to build new friendships and relationships, to find a community.

and so what else can we do?
  1. we need direct sunlight on our face, every day, or as close to every day, for at least half an hour.
    earlier in the day is better. more than half an hour, throughout the day, even better. specifically, direct bright sun on our face. shaded or indirect light is not enough. being near a bright window, while better than nothing, is not enough.
  2. we need to prioritize regular, intensive exercise, for mental health. physical health doesn’t even matter at this point. it also doesn’t matter if you don’t believe it will work. it will materially help.
  3. we will keep on looking for friends and people that we love, and love hanging out with.
  4. don’t forget to eat, and sleep.
  5. sometimes reading rilke, or the words of some others, can bring some comfort.

i am already woven with the rhythms of the universe.
everything is already here.
i am here.
