things i have learned

may 4, 2026

things I have learned:

  1. i can fall in love with someone in less than 7 days
  2. physical affection, especially if gentle and genuine, overrides everything else.
  3. i am like a moth to a light to a fascinating mind. better if their wanderings are weird - paths i have never encountered before, and continue to not be able to conceive of. better even, yet, if they have the a loving, kind, painfully generous heart.
  4. (addendum to above) this is strange, because i don’t think i’m this myself, especially my mind. unclear if i’d ever find a match, be good enough for the kind i’d like.
  5. still, there exists people out there for whom my desires fit like a key to a lock – who, astoundingly, want the same things I want: who pulls me closer in to them, all the time; who asks if they can carry me to bed; who, once there, falls in the same spell as i do into his; who teases me; who makes me laugh; who comes back, again, and again, and again
  6. if c was found by sheer luck; a by a stone’s toss; then surely it must follow that there will be others out there;
  7. i wish to be more alive in the way that they are - thinking, being, engaging with the world, laughing, playing, joy, so present
  8. a is an excellent writer
  9. a is a weirdo
  10. even when it’s clear that it’s not something that they want, or it’s something that they’re conflicted about, men seem to have a harder time calling it off, and then I have to do it for them.
  11. i am terrible at it, too.
