may 2025

how is it may already?

  • my brother’s graduation
  • deciding (feeling?) that i want to take a break / vacation after the apprenticeship, away from mcgriff
  • hitting up karen in hopes of a better apartment situation
  • starting clay class

emotionally - things are still a struggle. it’s becoming more and more clear that i do not like my job; that i struggle with emotional regulation and with not being swallowed or overwhelmed by my feelings; that i lack a strong and healthy connection with my inner child, which hampers my capacity to fully express myself, pursue my desires, experience joy. there is also, of course, the depression. to tackle any one of these alone would be a task, but to be presented with all of them at once –

and there is of course also the loneliness –

it is a struggle to stay focused, to persist, to balance the desire or necessity for self-work with the need for pursuing happiness and other forms of self-care (food, friends, interests) in the moments i have available energy and regulation.

i guess this is a common problem - the lack of enough spoons. i must constantly battle (or stay vigilant, or be open to and embrace) all of my feelings.

we are working slowly working towards shifting things, to a life of joy – to a life i like.
one big question is, what would i enjoy doing, for work?
