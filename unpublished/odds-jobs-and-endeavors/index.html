1. tech support, CG Jung Institute
09/2024 - 02/2025 (5 months)

helped various folks make sure their class was set up with zoom and the TV for presentations and meetings. after a while, getting up at 8am on Friday after a long week, just to help for half an hour on site, just wasn’t quite worth it anymore. i would often also get asked to help for a couple of prime weekend or holiday hours days before, and the scheduling for that just didn’t work out, either.
